Attendance not confirmed reminder

Until attendance is confirmed, parents cannot see it in the mobile app. Skipped on Sundays, holidays and when it is already confirmed.

Long absence report

Students absent several school days in a row (Sundays and holidays are not counted). If nobody is on the list, a cheerful "all clear" email is sent instead.

All-clear messages

When no student is on the long absence list, the report email carries one of these messages, picked at random (never the same one twice in a row). Use {days} for the absent-days number. At most 20 messages.

How it works
  • Every minuteThe system checks whether a notification of your school is due.
  • Attendance reminderSkips Sundays, holidays and days already confirmed. Otherwise it emails "not marked yet" or "saved but not confirmed", and once more after the gap you chose.
  • Long absence reportFinds students absent the chosen number of school days in a row, then sends the list, or an all-clear message.
  • If sending failsIt retries up to 3 times, 5 minutes apart, then stops for the day.
  • Once a dayEach notification goes out once a day, even after a server restart (a missed one still runs within 30 minutes).
